The chain of ideas behind the numbers on your phone

Every 0 to 9 on your keypad traveled more than 2,000 years to reach you. The chain starts in 326 BCE, when Alexander the Great reaches Punjab. Push the seven dominoes, watch each idea fly across the map, and solve a challenge to add a chapter to the Book of Mathematics.

Based on historian William Dalrymple's conversation on Al Jazeera's Déjà Vu and his book The Golden Road.
